Beta update 2

November 2026

More from your feedback: a piece can be finished with just its name, and supplies can be measured by length or weight.

New

  • Give a finished piece 1 to 5 stars for how well it came out, when you finish it or later on its page. It's never required, and tapping the same star again takes the grade off.
  • Work on it again, on a finished piece's page, puts it back on the Work screen with its clock running. Its time, notes, photo, and the prep and supplies it used all stay.
  • Give a supply bought by length or weight a size, like an 18 inch rod or a 1000 g bag. A piece then enters how much it used in that unit, like 3 inches, and gets that share of the cost. Correcting the size later keeps the amount each piece entered.
  • Put supplies in groups, like Color rods or Clear tubing. Pick a group from the ones you've saved, or add a new one.
  • Narrow a long supply list by group, or search it by name, on the Supplies screen and when adding a supply to a piece.

Improved

  • A name is all a finished piece needs. A photo and notes are optional, so Done then Save never makes you stop to type.
  • Counted prep is saved as a template unless it's lost, since that's how pieces pick it. Its Save as template switch is gone.
  • The guide that opens on first launch is shorter: four steps, starting with timing your work, each pointing at the button it's about. Tap ? on the Work screen to see it again.
  • A piece with no cost says No cost in its Cost column on the Pieces tab.
  • Each supply on the Supplies screen sits in its own box.
  • The unit list offers every unit you've used, for supplies and prep alike.
  • Before an update changes how your data is stored, EvenCraft saves a copy of it on your phone. If an update can't finish, it tells you to reopen the app, not to delete it.

Fixed

  • On a piece worked over more than one day, the dates in its day by day table no longer break into a letter per line.

Beta update 1

November 2026

Thank you to everyone testing EvenCraft. Every change in this update came from your feedback: fewer taps, and nothing that makes you stop working to fill something in.

New

  • Swipe a piece on the Work screen to pause it, rename it, mark it lost, or delete it.
  • Rename a piece while you're working on it. Its timer keeps running.
  • Swipe a piece on the Pieces tab to delete it without opening it first.
  • Swipe a template to remove it from your templates. The pieces you made from it stay.
  • A red Mark as lost button when you finish a piece or add past work, so a lost piece is one tap.

Improved

  • Done no longer opens the camera. Take a photo from the finish screen only when you want one.
  • The piece you're working on stays at the top of the Work screen, so a long list never pushes it out of sight.
  • You can add new prep when you finish a piece, even before you've added any supplies.
  • The Pieces tab shows each piece as a card with its photo, name, date, time worked, prep time, and cost.
  • A piece's page shows the prep and supplies it used the same way.
  • The Supplies list shows the price of one, the case it came in, and the day it was priced, each in its own place.
  • The two boxes for how much a prep made are now labeled Count and Unit.

Fixed

  • Words and numbers on the Pieces tab no longer break in the middle.
  • A long supply price no longer squeezes the priced date down to a letter per line.
  • Prices and amounts on a piece's page no longer spill onto a second line.
