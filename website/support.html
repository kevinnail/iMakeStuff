Common questions

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. There is no account and nothing is sent to a server. Deleting the app deletes its data, so keep iCloud Backup turned on if you want it backed up.

Does EvenCraft need an internet connection?

No. Everything works offline.

The camera or camera roll isn't working.

Open Settings > EvenCraft and turn on Camera, and allow adding to Photos if you want copies saved to your camera roll.

Is my data private?

EvenCraft collects nothing. Read the full privacy policy.

Have a question or found a bug?

Email me. Including your iPhone model and iOS version helps with any problem report.
