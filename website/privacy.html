Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026

EvenCraft does not collect any data. There is no account, no sign in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no server. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone.

What the app stores

The pieces you record, their times, breaks, notes, supplies, and photos are saved inside the app on your iPhone. None of it is sent to the developer or to anyone else, and the developer has no way to see it.

Camera and photos

  • The camera is used only when you take a photo of a piece. The photo is saved in the app, and a copy is added to your camera roll if you allow it. That permission only lets the app add photos, not view your library.
  • When you choose a photo from your library, the iOS photo picker gives the app only the photo you pick. The app cannot see the rest of your library.
  • You can change either permission at any time in Settings > EvenCraft.

Lock Screen timer

While a piece is running, iOS can show its timer on your Lock Screen. That timer is shown by your iPhone itself and nothing is sent anywhere.

Third parties

EvenCraft contains no third party analytics, advertising, or tracking tools, and shares no data with anyone.

Backups

If iCloud Backup or computer backups are turned on, iOS includes EvenCraft's data in those backups the same way it does for your other apps. Those backups are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, and the developer has no access to them.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your iPhone until you delete it. Deleting EvenCraft from your iPhone deletes all of its data. Because the developer never receives any of your data, there is nothing held elsewhere to request or delete.

Children

EvenCraft collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the support page.
